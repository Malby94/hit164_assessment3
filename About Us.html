About Us

Our story

At Urban Stitch, we started with a simple idea creating comfortable, stylish shirts inspired by two very different parts of Australia. Wade, from Brisbane, and Manono, from Darwin, brought together their different backgrounds and styles to build a brand focused on quality, individuality, and everyday wear. What began as conversations about fashion and creativity turned into Urban Stitch, an online business built on passion, hard work, and the belief that good clothing should feel personal, confident, and easy to wear anywhere.

Brand Mission

“We create affordable streetwear-inspired shirts designed for comfort and everyday wear.”

Manufacturing

At Urban Stitch, we are committed to producing sports shirts that combine quality, comfort, and responsible manufacturing practices. We work with suppliers who value fair working conditions, safe workplaces, and ethical production standards, while focusing on durable materials designed for performance and everyday wear. Every product is selected with attention to comfort, fit, and long-lasting quality to ensure our customers receive sportswear they can rely on. Our goal is to create apparel that not only looks good but is made with care, integrity, and respect for the people involved in the manufacturing process.
